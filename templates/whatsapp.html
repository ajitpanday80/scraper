Before you send

  • This uses unofficial WhatsApp Web automation. WhatsApp can ban the number. Use a dedicated SIM, never your personal number.
  • The daily limit ramps up automatically per number: 10 a day for 3 days, 20 until day 7, 30 until day 14, then 50. A new number restarts the ramp.
  • Replies like STOP or “not interested” add the person to the opt-out list automatically.
  • Warm up a new number first: chat normally with real contacts for a week or two before linking it here.
  • Messages go out one at a time with a random 3–4 minute gap, shared by every WhatsApp campaign. Keep “Messages per day” low (20–40) on a new number.
  • Numbers that are not on WhatsApp are skipped and logged as failed. They never pause a campaign.
  • Switching number keeps all history. The per-person limits still apply across numbers.
